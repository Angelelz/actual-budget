Added two tracking-budget actions for long-term planning. **Set as long-term** copies a spend category's current budget forward over the configured horizon (default 12 months); also available month-wide to copy every spend category at once. **Carry over from last month** adds last month's leftover (or subtracts overspending) to the current month's budget; also available month-wide. Both are exposed in the per-cell chevron menu and the per-month "..." menu, and both are restricted to spend categories (income is unchanged).
