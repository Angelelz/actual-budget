Added a **Debt projection** report (tracking budget). Pick accounts in priority order and the report walks future months forward — surplus (projected income minus projected spend) pours into the first account until it hits $0, then the next. Charts show one line per account, total debt over time, monthly surplus bars, and debt-free milestone callouts. The current month uses remaining budget (budget minus already-spent). Reachable at /reports/debt-projection.
